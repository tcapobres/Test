Why do some records say "BUY (RECONSTRUCTED)"? Hive Engine permanently deletes older market transactions from their public APIs. Because we know exactly who holds tokens from the Rich List, this app mathematically calculates the missing historical purchases and reconstructs them into the ledger, ensuring you have a 100% accurate count of the supply sold. We assume these pruned sales were executed at the Tranche 1 price (1.0 HIVE).
